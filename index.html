Who we are

What stays on your device

Everything you create or set up in the apps is stored locally on your phone. It is not uploaded to us. That includes your projects, files and their contents, preferences such as your theme, and a record of your purchase if you buy the app (product ID and transaction ID, kept in your device’s secure storage so the app can unlock without asking again).

Because we hold none of this, we cannot recover it if you uninstall an app or lose your phone. Export or back up your work if it matters to you.

Free with ads, or one-time purchase

Each app is free to download. You can use it free with ads, or pay once in Google Play to use it without ads. There is no subscription and no recurring charge. Ads are shown only in the free version.

Third-party services

The apps rely on outside services for ads and payments. Each has its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to read them. Features specific to one app may use more services, and those are listed in that app’s section.

Google AdMob (ads)

The free version of each app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and operating-system details, and information about how ads are displayed and interacted with. Ads may be personalized based on this data. Learn more in Google’s advertising policy and how AdMob uses data from apps.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. The purchased version shows no ads.

Google Play Billing (purchases)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google tells the app whether the purchase is valid, and you can restore it on a new device with the same Google account. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

  • On-device data stays until you delete it inside the app, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Ad data is held by Google under its own policies.
  • For anything held by a service you chose to use in LetsCode JS (an AI provider, Expo), see the retention list in the LetsCode JS section.

Your choices and rights

  • Ads: on Android, open Settings > Privacy > Ads (the path varies by device) to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization. You can also manage ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Remove ads entirely: buy the one-time purchase in the app. It is a single payment, not a subscription.
  • Legal rights: depending on where you live (for example under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the GDPR, or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Because we hold almost no personal information about you, most of it sits with Google and the other services above. Contact them for data they hold, or contact us and we will help where we can.

Children

The LetsCode apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through an app, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Security

We store sensitive items such as your purchase record in your device’s secure storage. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keeping your device locked and up to date helps protect everything in the apps.

LetsCode JS: extra features

LetsCode JS is a code editor for building and previewing projects on your phone in React, React Native, Vue, Angular or vanilla JavaScript. In addition to everything above, it does the following.

Extra data stored on your device

  • Projects: installed package lists and which project you last had open.
  • Assets you add: images, audio, video or other files you choose with the photo or file picker. A copy is saved inside the app’s storage.
  • Expo access token (optional): if you sign in to Expo, the token is saved in your device’s secure storage (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain).
  • AI settings (optional): your API key for each provider, your chosen provider and model, and, for a custom server, its address, all in secure storage.
  • AI chat history (optional): the last 40 messages of each project’s AI chat, as text only. This includes what you typed, which may include code or errors you pasted, and the AI’s explanations. Strings that look like API keys are automatically hidden. Chat history is deleted when you delete the project or tap “Clear chat”.
  • AI notices: simple yes/no records of which first-use AI notices you accepted. For custom servers we store only a short hash of the address, not the address itself.

Optional AI assistant

The AI assistant is inactive until you add your own API key from a provider you choose. If you never add a key, the app makes no AI requests.

  • You choose a provider: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini, or a custom server that follows the OpenAI format. You paste your own API key.
  • Requests go directly from your device to that provider, using your key. They do not pass through our servers. We never see your key, your prompts, your code or the replies.
  • A request is sent only when you tap Send (or confirm the first-use notice that follows your Send tap). Opening the AI screens, “Start with AI” and “Fix with AI” only fill in the input box.
  • The AI never changes your project on its own. You review each proposed change before it is applied, and you can undo it.

What is sent to the provider:

  • Your prompt, and for “Fix with AI” the error text from the preview.
  • The project name, framework, entry file and installed package names and versions.
  • The project’s file list, asset file names (never asset contents), and the contents of the files included in the request, up to about 60,000 characters. The app tells you which files were left out or shortened.
  • Your last few earlier prompts and the AI’s explanations from the same chat, as text.
  • Your API key, in a request header, so the provider can authorize the request.

No device identifiers, advertising ID or analytics data are added by the AI feature. Because project code may be sent, do not include passwords, secret keys or personal information in files you send to an AI provider.

Providers, billing and their policies:

  • The provider you choose processes your request under its own terms and privacy policy: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google. For a custom server, whoever runs that server receives your prompts and code, and we do not control it.
  • Some providers offer a free tier, and some paid plans may handle data differently. For example, on Google’s free tier your prompts and code may be used to improve Google products. Check your provider’s terms before sending private code.
  • API usage is billed by your provider if your plan is paid. This is separate from any ChatGPT or Claude subscription, which cannot be used in this app.
  • When an AI reply suggests npm packages, the app checks that each package name exists on the npm registry. Only the package names are sent.

Reporting a reply: each AI reply has a “Report” option. It opens a pre-filled email addressed to us containing the reply text and the provider name. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email. If you do, we receive the reply text and your email address, and we use them only to review the report.

Package and library services

Some features load code from the internet while you work:

  • npm registry (registry.npmjs.org): when you search for packages, your search text is sent to npm. See npm’s privacy policy.
  • esm.sh: used to fetch package files for the editor, for installed packages, and for previews. The package names and versions you request are sent to it.
  • Tailwind CDN (cdn.tailwindcss.com): loaded in previews and exported pages that use Tailwind.

Like any website or CDN, these services can see your IP address and basic request details. We do not control their logs. Some packages may be cached on your device so previews work offline.

Expo (optional)

These features only run when you use them. See Expo’s privacy policy.

  • Expo account sign-in: when you paste an access token, the app sends it to Expo’s API (api.expo.dev) to confirm it is valid and to read your Expo username. We do not receive the token.
  • Device preview for React Native: the app uploads your project’s files and dependency list to Expo Snack. The upload is anonymous (not tied to your Expo account), but anyone who has the resulting link or QR code can open the project. Do not put secrets, API keys or personal information in a project you preview this way.

Features that share your work

  • Live preview over Wi-Fi: the app can serve your project from your phone so another device on the same network can open it. This uses plain HTTP, and anyone on that network who has the address can view the preview while it is running. Use it only on networks you trust, and stop the session when you are done.
  • Export and share: when you export a project, the app creates a file and opens your phone’s share sheet. What happens next depends on the app you choose.
  • Copy to clipboard: copying an error message or a preview link puts it on your clipboard only when you tap the copy button.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load packages, show ads, run previews and check connectivity.
  • Photos and files: only when you choose to add an image or file to a project. You pick the files; the app does not browse your library on its own.

LetsCode JS does not use your contacts, microphone, camera or precise location.

Retention and deletion in LetsCode JS

  • Expo token: sign out of Expo inside the app to remove it from your device. You can also revoke the token in your Expo account settings.
  • AI key: in AI Settings, tap “Remove key” to delete a saved key from your device. You can also revoke the key in your provider’s dashboard.
  • AI chat history: tap “Clear chat”, delete the project, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Data held by your AI provider is controlled by that provider. Contact them to ask about their retention or deletion.
  • Expo Snack uploads are held by Expo, not by us. To ask for removal, contact Expo.
